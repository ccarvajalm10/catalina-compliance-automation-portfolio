# Information Security Policy

**Owner:** CISO · **Approved by:** CEO and Board · **Version:** 3.1
**Approved:** 2025-02-10 · **Next review:** 2026-02-10 · **Review cycle:** annual or on significant change

## Purpose
Northwind Cloud Ltd operates a multi-tenant cloud analytics platform. This policy sets
the direction and principles for protecting the confidentiality, integrity and
availability of company and customer information.

## Scope
All employees, contractors, systems, and information assets within the Information
Security Management System (ISMS) boundary, which covers the SaaS platform, its
supporting AWS infrastructure (eu-west-1), corporate Google Workspace, and the London office.

## Principles
1. Information is classified and handled according to the Asset Management Policy.
2. Access follows least privilege and is reviewed quarterly.
3. Security risks are assessed at least annually using the documented risk method and
   tracked in the risk register to acceptance by the risk owner.
4. All staff complete security awareness training on hire and annually.
5. Suppliers handling customer data are assessed before onboarding.
6. Incidents are reported and managed under the Incident Response Plan.

## Governance
The Information Security Steering Committee (CISO, CTO, Head of People, Head of Legal)
meets quarterly, reviews KPIs, risks, incidents and audit results, and records minutes.
The CISO reports ISMS performance to the Board twice per year.

## Policy framework
This policy is supported by: Access Control Policy, Asset Management Policy, HR Security
Policy, Supplier Security Policy, Secure Development Policy, Cryptography Policy,
Business Continuity & Backup Policy, Acceptable Use Policy, and the Incident Response Plan.
All are published on the company wiki and staff acknowledge them on hire.

## Compliance
Violations may result in disciplinary action. Exceptions require documented CISO approval
with an expiry date.
