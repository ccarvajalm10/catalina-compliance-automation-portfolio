# HR Security Policy

**Owner:** Head of People · **Version:** 1.3 · **Approved:** 2024-09-01 · **Next review:** 2025-09-01

## Screening
Prior to employment, all candidates undergo identity verification, right-to-work checks,
and two reference checks. Candidates for roles with production access additionally undergo
a basic criminal record check where local law permits.

## Terms of employment
Employment contracts include an information security clause and reference the Acceptable
Use Policy. Contractors sign an equivalent schedule in their statement of work.

## Confidentiality
All employees and contractors sign a confidentiality agreement before their start date.
The agreement is reviewed by Legal every two years.

## Awareness and training
- New joiners complete security induction in their first week.
- All staff complete annual security awareness training (KnowBe4); completion is tracked
  by People Ops and reported to the Steering Committee.
- Engineers complete secure-coding training annually.
- Simulated phishing exercises run quarterly.

## Offboarding
People Ops triggers the offboarding workflow, which notifies IT to remove access and
recover assets. The employee is reminded in the exit meeting of continuing confidentiality
obligations.

> Disciplinary handling of security violations is managed case-by-case by People Ops and
> the employee's manager. There is no written disciplinary procedure specific to security.
