# Supplier Security Policy

**Owner:** Head of Legal · **Version:** 0.9 (draft) · **Last updated:** 2025-01-20

## Onboarding
Before a supplier is granted access to Confidential or Customer Data, the requesting team
completes a supplier risk questionnaire and Legal reviews the supplier's security
documentation (SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certificate where available).

## Contracts
Data processing agreements are signed with all suppliers that process personal data.
Security requirements are included in the master services agreement template.

## Cloud services
The primary infrastructure provider is AWS. Use of new cloud services that will store
Customer Data must be approved by the CISO.

## Ongoing management
Critical suppliers are reviewed annually. The supplier register lists each supplier, the
data they access, and the date of last review.

> **Known gaps (to close before certification):**
> - The ICT supply-chain risk (sub-processors of our sub-processors) is not formally assessed.
> - There is no defined process for monitoring supplier service levels or handling supplier-side changes.
> - The policy is still in draft and has not been formally approved.
