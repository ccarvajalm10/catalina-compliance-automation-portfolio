# Acceptable Use & Remote Working Policy

**Owner:** IT Manager · **Version:** 2.1 · **Approved:** 2025-02-20 · **Next review:** 2026-02-20

## Acceptable use
- Company devices and accounts are for business use; incidental personal use must not
  affect security or performance.
- Prohibited: disabling security software, installing unapproved software with admin rights,
  connecting unmanaged devices to production, sharing credentials.
- Customer Data may only be stored in approved systems, never on local disks or personal cloud storage.

## Remote working
Northwind Cloud is remote-first; most staff work from home.
- Only MDM-managed, encrypted devices may access company systems.
- Access is via SSO with MFA; production access additionally requires the company VPN and a hardware key.
- Staff must use a private, password-protected network and lock screens when away.
- Home working environments must keep company information out of view of others (clear screen).

## Clear desk / clear screen
Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes. Printed Confidential material must not be left
unattended and must be shredded when no longer needed. The London office has lockable storage.

## Monitoring
Endpoint and SSO activity is logged for security purposes in line with the privacy notice to staff.
